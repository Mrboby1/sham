Tips

  • Whistle clearly, one note at a time, close to the phone. Humming works too.
  • For a repeated note, take a tiny breath between the two.
  • Whistles are high, so the tune is moved down into the piano's middle. Use − / + to change the octave.
  • You don't need perfect pitch: it finds your own tuning and snaps to the nearest notes.
