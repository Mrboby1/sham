How it works. Every morning, headlines and short summaries from about 40 outlets in 14+ countries are collected, from left to right, Western, Russian, Chinese, Gulf, Israeli and others. Articles about the same event are grouped. For the stories that matter for markets, the page shows what all sides agree on, what is disputed and by whom, and how each side frames it. From that comes the most likely version of reality and dated forecasts with a probability.

Keeping score. Every forecast is checked after its date: right or wrong. The Brier score measures accuracy: 0 is perfect, and 0.25 is what you'd get by always saying 50%.

Only headlines and the outlets' own summaries are used, with links to the original articles. Forecasts are estimates, not certainties. Not financial advice.
